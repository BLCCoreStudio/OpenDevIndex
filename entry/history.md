# History

- **2016** — The public PyTorch repository was created as the framework was being prepared for release.
- **2017** — PyTorch was publicly introduced and rapidly adopted in the machine-learning research community.
- PyTorch expanded from its tensor/autograd core into compilation, export, distributed training, mixed precision, and broader accelerator support.
- **2026** — Current documentation continues to center tensors and autograd while exposing extensive compiler, distributed, and accelerator APIs.

Upstream release notes are the source of truth for version-specific changes.
