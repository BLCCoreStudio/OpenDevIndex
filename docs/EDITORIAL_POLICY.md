# Editorial policy

OpenDevIndex is a source-backed technical index. Inclusion is not an endorsement, sponsorship, or product ranking.

## Evidence

Prefer primary and canonical references:

1. official project or standards documentation;
2. canonical source repositories;
3. original security advisories or research;
4. reputable secondary material only when primary material is insufficient.

Generated text, search snippets, social posts, and marketing copy are not treated as authoritative sources on their own.

## Accuracy

Technical claims should be specific enough to verify and conservative enough to remain useful. Avoid unsupported superlatives, invented benchmarks, popularity claims without evidence, and assumptions about licensing or security.

A reachable link is not proof that a claim is correct. Source Health checks availability; editorial review checks whether the cited material actually supports the entry.

## Classification

Use [`docs/TAXONOMY.md`](TAXONOMY.md). Stable module addresses and semantic taxonomy are separate. New entries should choose the best canonical kind and use domains for cross-cutting areas such as AI, security, cloud, data, accessibility, media, and observability.

## Verification dates

`verified_at` records when catalog metadata was reviewed against its cited sources. It is not a guarantee that every upstream project changed on that date. Fast-moving entries should be reviewed more frequently, and stale verification dates are intended to become visible through maintenance tooling.

## Corrections

Corrections are welcome when they include a clear explanation and, where possible, a primary source. Material factual errors should be corrected promptly without waiting for a scheduled content milestone.

## Neutrality

OpenDevIndex does not rank technologies based on sponsorship, affiliate relationships, or commercial pressure. Alternatives and trade-offs should be described neutrally when they materially help developers make decisions.
